## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up. Your trackers, lists, and settings stay on your phone.

### How does the trial work?
Eligible new subscribers can start a 14-day free trial from the paywall by tapping Start free trial. When it ends, a yearly or monthly subscription begins unless you cancel first. To avoid being charged, cancel before it ends in your App Store or Google Play account settings. On the App Store, cancel at least 24 hours before.

### What can I do without a subscription?
You can look around everything, archive and unarchive lists, end an open-ended tracker, and delete trackers, lists, and tasks. Creating, ticking, editing, and reminders need a subscription.

### How do I restore my subscription?
Tap Restore purchases on the Welcome screen, on the paywall, or in Settings. Your subscription belongs to your App Store or Google Play account, so it doesn't carry over between iPhone and Android.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
Cancel from your store account, or from Settings &rarr; Manage subscription in the app. On iPhone, go to Settings &rarr; your name &rarr; Subscriptions. On Android, open the Play Store &rarr; Subscriptions. The subscription stays active until the current billing period ends.

### What happens to my data if I stop subscribing?
Everything you made stays on your phone, and you can still see it all. Creating, ticking, and editing need a subscription again, and reminders stop.

### Why didn't a reminder arrive?
Check that notifications are on for ToDoTracker in your phone's settings, and that Pause all reminders is off in the app's Settings. When notifications are off, the app's Settings shows a link to turn them on. Reminders don't arrive once today is ticked, or outside the tracker's dates and pauses. On iPhone, keep Background App Refresh on for the app. On Android, a reminder may arrive a few minutes late, and battery saving can delay it more. If you haven't opened the app for about a week, Android may deliver only one reminder a day until you open it again.

### How do I move to a new phone?
The app has no account or export. Your data moves with your phone's own backup or transfer: iCloud Backup or Quick Start on iPhone, and Google backup or the transfer during setup on Android. It can't move between iPhone and Android. After moving, tap Restore purchases to restore your subscription.

### How do I delete my data?
Delete a tracker, list, or task in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything. Copies in your phone's own backup stay there until you delete that backup.
