# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what the ToDoTracker app ("the App", "we", "us") does with your information. FatCatSolutions ("the Developer") provides the App.

ToDoTracker keeps your data on your device. There is no account system, so you never sign up and never give us your name, email, phone number, or a password. We don't run a server that stores your trackers, lists, or tasks, and we don't build advertising profiles.

## 1. Data stored only on your device

Everything you enter or create lives in the App's private storage on your device:

- Your trackers: their names, dates, ticked days, attempts, pauses, strict-streak setting, and reminder times
- Your to-do lists and their tasks, including which tasks are done and which lists are archived
- App settings, such as the theme, the first day of the week, and whether all reminders are paused
- Subscription flags the App needs to work: whether you have seen the Welcome screen, whether you have subscribed before, and whether a purchase is waiting for approval. On Android the App also keeps the end date of your free trial, so it can show it after a restart
- Device-only records, such as which reminders are scheduled and whether the App has asked for notification permission

The App works offline. This data stays on your device unless your device's own backup copies it (see section 2). Deleting a tracker, list, or task in the App removes it. Deleting the App removes everything, unless your device backup kept a copy.

## 2. Your device's own backup

The App has no backup or sync feature of its own and makes no backup network calls. If you have turned on iCloud Backup (iPhone) or Google backup (Android), your device includes the App's data in that backup, and in a transfer to a new phone. You control this in your device settings. On Android, the device-only records from section 1 are left out.

These backups are stored in your own Apple or Google account and fall under Apple's or Google's privacy policies. We can't access them.

## 3. Diagnostics

The App uses Sentry to collect crash reports, error reports, and reports of the App freezing, so we can fix bugs. A report may include your device model, operating-system version, App version, technical details such as stack traces, the App's recent technical log messages, and a random installation ID that Sentry's software creates on your device. The App tells Sentry not to collect your IP address or other personal details, and never attaches screenshots or screen layouts. Reports are designed to leave out your tracker names, list titles, and task text. We don't use reports to identify you.

The App has no usage analytics. It sends no statistics about how you use it.

## 4. Subscriptions

Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) processes subscriptions. The App never handles payments and we never receive or store your card details. The store takes care of billing, renewals, and refunds under its own privacy policy. The App only asks the store for prices and your subscription status.

## 5. Device permissions

- Notifications: asked only when you save a tracker that has reminders, or when you tap Allow on the message explaining that reminders need it. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us.
- Background refresh (iPhone) and running after a restart (Android): used only to reschedule your reminders, for example after the phone restarts or the time zone changes.
- Network state (Android): used only to reload subscription prices when the connection comes back.

The App doesn't ask for your camera, photos, location, contacts, microphone, or health data.

You can deny or withdraw any of these permissions in your device settings. The rest of the App keeps working, but reminders need notifications.

## 6. Third parties we share data with

| Recipient | Purpose | Their policy |
|---|---|---|
| Sentry | Crash and error diagnostics | sentry.io/privacy |
| Apple / Google | Process subscription purchases | apple.com / google.com policies |

We don't sell your personal data, and the App has no advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking SDKs.

Links in the App, such as Terms, Privacy, Manage subscription, and Rate the app, open your browser or the store. What happens there falls under that site's or store's policy.

## 7. Data retention

- On-device data stays until you delete it or uninstall the App.
- Device backups stay in your Apple or Google account under their rules.
- Crash reports are kept under Sentry's data-retention policy.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or the California CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict how it is processed. We keep no accounts and your data is on your device, so you can do most of this yourself: edit or delete your trackers, lists, and tasks in the App, or uninstall it. For anything else, email us at the address below and we will answer within the time applicable law requires.

## 9. Children's privacy

The App is made for a general audience and is not aimed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the minimum age in your country. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Security

Your data sits in the App's private storage on your device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information. Protect your device with a passcode, because anyone who can open the App can see your data.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When something material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
